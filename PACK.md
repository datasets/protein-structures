## discord

**New dataset: protein structures, measured and predicted**

In 2025, four years after AlphaFold DB opened with 365,198 predicted structures, the Protein Data Bank released 17,236 new experimentally determined structures containing protein, more than in any earlier year and up from 13,820 in 2020. So far in 2026 (to 18 November) it has released 16,047.

AlphaFold DB's September 2025 release held 241,070,489 predictions, about 933 times the 258,244 experimental protein structures in the whole PDB. That is a huge expansion in coverage, but predictions are computed models, not measurements. Meanwhile the way structures are measured has changed: cryo-electron microscopy produced 41.6% of new protein structures in 2025, against 2.4% in 2015.

First comment: I work on DataHub. This counts experimentally determined PDB entries by year of first release and RCSB's experimental-method grouping, from the RCSB Search API (retrieved 22 November 2026), plus the AlphaFold DB size at each release from EBI's changelog.

Some numbers: 17,236 new entries with protein in 2025, the most of any year, against 13,820 in 2020; 16,047 so far in 2026 (to 18 November). Cryo-EM was 41.6% of 2025's new protein structures, 2.4% in 2015. AlphaFold DB's September 2025 release had 241,070,489 predictions, about 933 times the PDB's 258,244 experimental protein entries.

Caveats: counts are of current entries, so obsoleted ones are missing and past years may be slightly low; an entry is not a distinct protein; 2026 is partial; and the AlphaFold comparison is of counts, not like-for-like. Corrections welcome.
